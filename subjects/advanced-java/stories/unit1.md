# Unit 1 story: GUI Programming (hero: Mo the Mad Mouse)

1. Mo sits on a light **swing** watching **Little Pigs Make Jam**. Then **Four Cats Sit Closely Very** build Mo a house. *(Swing: Lightweight, Pluggable look, MVC, J-names · Frame: Create, Size, Close-op, Visible)*
2. Mo paints the walls with **Sad Dogs Run**, and **Lazy Rabbits Eat** the lines, boxes and circles in **Really Good Banana** colours. *(Super, Draw, Repaint · Line2D, Rectangle2D, Ellipse2D · RGB)*
3. The doorbell rings: **Some Elephants Listen**, and the **Ice Reaches Ocean**. Mo gets mad and **Kicks Ice Windows**, so **Pink Giraffes** bring **Pizza Time Rocks**. *(Source→Event→Listener · Implement, Register, Override · Action, Mouse, MouseMotion, Key, Item, Window · Pressed, Typed, Released)*
4. A lazy helper with a **Big Old Nose** fixes only one window. *(Adapter: Blank bodies, Override one, No empty methods)*
5. Mo opens **My Village Cafe**. **Funny Bears Get Cake** at tables **North, East, South, West**, and **Lazy Bears Teach Tiny Cats Running Circles**. *(Model, View, Controller · Flow, Border, Grid, Card · NESW + Center · JLabel, JButton, JTextField, JTextArea, JCheckBox, JRadioButton, JComboBox)*
